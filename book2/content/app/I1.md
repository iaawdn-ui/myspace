# Appendix I: Acronyms, Study Guide and Frequently Asked Questions

This appendix supports successful study and assessment in CAC. 702. It is designed to be used, not merely read: photocopy, adapt within local policy and complete the materials in real cases, under supervision, throughout the course. Materials are introduced with the developmental reasoning that justifies them, because a form detached from its reasoning is soon completed mechanically and read uncritically.

## Purpose and Use

Second, developmental knowledge disciplines the choice of method. The counsellor considers the adolescent's capacity for abstraction, tolerance for ambiguity, emotional vocabulary, attention span, and position in the family before selecting how to work, not after the method has failed. Early adolescents generally need shorter inferential chains, more activity, more structure and more frequent summary; older adolescents can usually sustain reflective, verbal and future-oriented work, provided trust is established. Across all ages, explanation must be offered in language the young person can repeat back to a parent or friend, because an explanation that cannot leave the room has limited developmental power.

The appendix is organised around six resources: course acronyms, examination blueprint, portfolio checklist, revision timetable, frequently asked questions, further study pathways. Each is presented with instructions, the developmental rationale for its design, cautions about misuse and a worked fragment showing competent completion.

### Course acronyms

The resource on **course acronyms** exists because successful study and assessment in CAC. 702. Complete it at the point in the work where its questions are live, not at intake merely because it is in the file; review it whenever the trajectory changes; and record, each time it is used, what decision it influenced. A resource that never changes a decision is administrative decoration and should be shortened or discarded.
