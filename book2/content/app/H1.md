# Appendix H: Referral, Safeguarding and Crisis Pathways

This appendix supports timely referral, safeguarding action and crisis response for adolescents. It is designed to be used, not merely read: photocopy, adapt within local policy and complete the materials in real cases, under supervision, throughout the course. Materials are introduced with the developmental reasoning that justifies them, because a form detached from its reasoning is soon completed mechanically and read uncritically.

## Purpose and Use

Second, developmental knowledge disciplines the choice of method. The counsellor considers the adolescent's capacity for abstraction, tolerance for ambiguity, emotional vocabulary, attention span, and position in the family before selecting how to work, not after the method has failed. Early adolescents generally need shorter inferential chains, more activity, more structure and more frequent summary; older adolescents can usually sustain reflective, verbal and future-oriented work, provided trust is established. Across all ages, explanation must be offered in language the young person can repeat back to a parent or friend, because an explanation that cannot leave the room has limited developmental power.

The appendix is organised around six resources: referral thresholds, safeguarding escalation, crisis assessment pathway, inter-agency communication, safety planning contacts record, post-incident follow-up. Each is presented with instructions, the developmental rationale for its design, cautions about misuse and a worked fragment showing competent completion.

## The Resources in Outline

### Referral thresholds

The resource on **referral thresholds** exists because timely referral, safeguarding action and crisis response for adolescents. Complete it at the point in the work where its questions are live, not at intake merely because it is in the file; review it whenever the trajectory changes; and record, each time it is used, what decision it influenced. A resource that never changes a decision is administrative decoration and should be shortened or discarded. Where the resource is shared with adolescents, families or schools, share it in a form and at a pace they can genuinely use, explain what will happen to the information, and record consent and assent arrangements in Appendix A documentation.
