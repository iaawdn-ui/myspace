# Appendix F (continued): Worksheets and Session Tools for Adolescents

## Worked Guidance

A final property of mechanism is that it is almost always relational. Even processes that appear to be purely internal, such as private reasoning, rumination or self-talk, were typically learned in relationships, are triggered by relationships and are revised in relationships. This is encouraging news for counsellors, because the counselling relationship is itself a developmental environment: a bounded, reliable, attentive relationship in which an adolescent can practise disclosure, disagreement, reflection and repair. When this book describes a technique, therefore, the student should always ask two questions: what process is this technique intended to set in motion, and what in the relationship makes that process possible for this particular adolescent at this particular age.

Timing converts identical events into different developmental experiences. Entering puberty earlier or later than peers, changing schools at twelve rather than fourteen, losing a parent before rather than after a network of friendships is established: each alters what the event means and what resources are available to meet it. Counsellors should routinely construct a simple developmental timeline with adolescents and families, marking transitions, losses, moves, illnesses, achievements and changes of school. The exercise is inexpensive, often moving, and routinely reveals the coincidence of demands that no single-informant account had made visible.

## Common Errors

Three errors recur when these materials are used. The first is mechanical completion: every box filled, no judgement exercised, and the resulting document mistaken for an assessment. The second is premature sharing: information passed to schools or agencies before the adolescent understands and, where lawful, agrees to what will be shared and why. The third is fossilisation: forms completed once and never revisited although the adolescent, the family and the difficulty have all changed. Each error is prevented by the same discipline — date everything, review everything against trajectory, and let the adolescent see and question what is recorded about them wherever policy permits.

## Quality Standard

Work in this appendix reaches graduate standard when a reader who does not know the case can reconstruct, from the documents alone, what was known, what was decided, on what developmental reasoning, with whose agreement, and what happened next. Audit your own files against that standard twice each term.
