# Preface

This book is the principal textbook for **CAC. 702 Adolescent Development**, a 1.6-unit course in the Master of Arts in Children and Adolescent Counseling at Triune Biblical University Global Extension. It follows CAC. 701, in which students were introduced to the theories of counselling, and it supplies the developmental knowledge without which those theories cannot be applied responsibly to young people. A counsellor may command a technique and still fail an adolescent by asking for reasoning the adolescent cannot yet perform, disclosing to adults in ways that destroy trust, or treating as defiance what is in fact an ordinary developmental task meeting an unready environment. This book exists to prevent those failures.

The book is organised in eight parts and fifty-eight chapters, followed by nine appendices. Parts I to VII build the developmental science: foundations and theory; biology and health; cognition and learning; identity, self and social cognition; emotion and relationships; risk and resilience; and diversity and life contexts. Part VIII converts that knowledge into assessment, formulation, matched intervention, school and family partnership, prevention, ethics and the assessment of students' own learning. Every chapter follows the same architecture — objectives, overview, concepts, mechanisms, trajectories, contexts, evidence, counselling implications, a case, assessment and intervention guidance, cultural and ethical analysis, key terms, summary, review questions, practice activities, a full practice laboratory with an extended worked case, advanced seminar exercises and annotated readings — so that students always know where they are and lecturers can assign with confidence.

The manuscript contains [[WORDCOUNT]] words as counted at production, together with diagrams and illustrated plates throughout. It is written as a lecturer teaches: directly, with argument, with worked examples, and with repeated insistence that developmental claims be observable, testable and revisable. It contains no confessional or devotional content; its authorities are the peer-reviewed developmental literature, the ethical codes of the counselling profession and the observable experience of adolescents, families and schools.

A word about how to read it. Developmental science is cumulative in this text: the ecological framework of Chapter 6, the brain development of Chapter 10, the identity work of Chapter 23 and the resilience research of Chapter 44 are load-bearing for everything that follows. Students reading for examination should therefore read in order at least once. Practitioners returning to the book with a case in mind may enter at any chapter, since each is written to stand alone, but are urged to read the Practice Laboratory of the chapter they use, because that is where the chapter's reasoning is shown working at full professional stretch.
