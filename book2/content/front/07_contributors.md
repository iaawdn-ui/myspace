# Contributors and Acknowledgements

This textbook was developed by the course team for the Master of Arts in Children and Adolescent Counseling, drawing on the peer-reviewed literature of developmental psychology, developmental neuroscience, education and counselling, and on the ethical and practice frameworks of the international counselling profession.

The course team thanks the school counsellors, teachers, youth workers, parents and, above all, the adolescents whose questions, objections and experience, encountered across many settings, taught the authors what a textbook on adolescence must actually answer. Their contributions are present throughout in composite and anonymised form.

Thanks are also due to the programme's external reviewers for scrutiny of the assessment materials, to the librarians who verified the reading pathways of Appendix E, and to the students of earlier cohorts whose seminar work exposed every unclear passage that survived into print, most of which, in consequence, did not.
