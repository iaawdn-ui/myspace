# 16-Week Topic Outlines — 1ST SEMESTER
## Master of Marriage and Family Therapy — Triune Biblical University Global Extension, USA

Each course runs 16 weeks. Week 8 is a mid-term checkpoint and Week 16 is the final integration/assessment week. The **Style** column points to the recommended presentation style number from `01-Presentation-Style-Prompts.md` for that week's lecture video.
