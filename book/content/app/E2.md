# Appendix E2: Annotated Bibliography (Systems, Development, Trauma, Culture, and Professional Practice)

## Family, Attachment, and Interpersonal Works

**Ainsworth, M. D. S., Blehar, M. C., Waters, E., & Wall, S. (1978). *Patterns of Attachment*.** Foundational strange situation research. Read with cross-cultural critiques.

**Asen, E., & Fonagy, P. (2021). *Mentalization-Based Treatment with Families*.** Practical family MBT using curiosity, affect, perspective taking, and network meetings.

**Bowlby, J. (1988). *A Secure Base*.** Clinically accessible attachment essays on caregiving, loss, and therapeutic relationships.

**Goldenberg, I., Stanton, M., & Goldenberg, H. (2017). *Family Therapy: An Overview* (9th ed.).** Broad family therapy survey with models, techniques, and research.

**Haley, J. (1976). *Problem-Solving Therapy*.** Strategic family therapy text historically important for directives, hierarchy, and sequences.

**McGoldrick, M., Gerson, R., & Petry, S. (2008). *Genograms: Assessment and Intervention* (3rd ed.).** Practical guide to family diagrams and intergenerational patterns.

**Minuchin, S. (1974). *Families and Family Therapy*.** Foundational structural family therapy text.

**Mufson, L., Dorta, K. P., Moreau, D., & Weissman, M. M. (2011). *Interpersonal Psychotherapy for Depressed Adolescents* (2nd ed.).** Main IPT-A manual linking depression to grief, disputes, transitions, and deficits.

**Weissman, M. M., Markowitz, J. C., & Klerman, G. L. (2017). *The Guide to Interpersonal Psychotherapy* (updated ed.).** Concise IPT overview across applications.

## Trauma, Emotion, and Somatic Works

**Cohen, J. A., Mannarino, A. P., & Deblinger, E. (2017). *Treating Trauma and Traumatic Grief in Children and Adolescents* (2nd ed.).** Core TF-CBT text with caregiver components and developmental adaptations.

**Dana, D. (2018). *The Polyvagal Theory in Therapy*.** Clinically accessible polyvagal-informed exercises and language. Pair with scientific critiques of polyvagal claims.

**Greenberg, L. S. (2015). *Emotion-Focused Therapy: Coaching Clients to Work Through Their Feelings* (2nd ed.).** Updated EFT account of emotion schemes, markers, and tasks.

**Herman, J. L. (2015). *Trauma and Recovery* (2015 ed.).** Influential phase-oriented and political account of trauma, safety, remembrance, and reconnection.

**Ogden, P., & Fisher, J. (2015). *Sensorimotor Psychotherapy*.** Clinical methods for tracking sensation, posture, movement, and procedural patterns.

**Porges, S. W. (2011). *The Polyvagal Theory*.** Primary theoretical source. Students should also read critiques and current autonomic science.

**Shapiro, F. (2018). *Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) Therapy* (3rd ed.).** Main EMDR text. Use certified training and child protocols before clinical delivery.

**van der Kolk, B. (2014). *The Body Keeps the Score*.** Influential synthesis of trauma, development, body, and treatment. Popular reach makes critical appraisal especially important.

## Child Development, Resilience, and Schools

**Blakemore, S.-J. (2018). *Inventing Ourselves*.** Accessible account of adolescent brain and social development.

**Bronfenbrenner, U. (1979). *The Ecology of Human Development*.** Primary ecological systems text.

**Dahl, R. E., Allen, N. B., Wilbrecht, L., & Suleiman, A. B. (2018).** “Importance of investing in adolescence from a developmental science perspective.” *Nature, 554*, 441–450. A concise argument for adolescence as a sensitive developmental window.

**Masten, A. S. (2014). *Ordinary Magic: Resilience in Development*.** Comprehensive resilience synthesis emphasising ordinary adaptive systems.

**Steinberg, L. (2023). *Adolescence* (13th ed.).** Standard developmental text integrating biology, cognition, family, peers, school, and culture.

**Ungar, M. (2011).** “The social ecology of resilience.” *American Journal of Orthopsychiatry, 81*(1), 1–17. Challenges individualistic resilience definitions.

**American School Counselor Association. (2022). *ASCA National Model* (4th ed.).** Comprehensive school counselling framework requiring local adaptation.

**Stone, C. B. (2022). *Ethics and Law for School Counselors* (5th ed.).** Detailed school confidentiality, records, and legal dilemmas.

## Culture, Feminist, Intersectional, and LGBTQ+ Practice

**American Psychological Association. (2015).** Guidelines for psychological practice with transgender and gender nonconforming people. Competencies for assessment, identity, stigma, and care systems.

**American Psychological Association. (2021).** Guidelines for psychological practice with sexual minority persons. Comprehensive affirmative practice resource.

**Crenshaw, K. (1989).** “Demarginalizing the intersection of race and sex.” Foundational intersectionality article grounded in Black feminist legal critique.

**Hook, J. N., et al. (2013).** Cultural humility research linking openness and alliance. Useful corrective to competence as static mastery.

**Meyer, I. H. (2003).** Minority stress model for lesbian, gay, and bisexual populations. Extend cautiously and intersectionally to other groups.

**Ratts, M. J., et al. (2016).** Multicultural and Social Justice Counseling Competencies. Framework linking self-awareness, client worldview, relationship, and advocacy.

**Ryan, C., et al. (2009).** Family rejection and health outcomes research. Pair with Family Acceptance Project practice materials.

**Sue, D. W., Sue, D., Neville, H. A., & Smith, L. (2022). *Counseling the Culturally Diverse* (9th ed.).** Major multicultural counselling text; read with attention to evolving terminology and evidence.

**Worell, J., & Remer, P. (2003). *Feminist Perspectives in Therapy*.** Empowerment model integrating gender, power, and social change.

## Motivational, Assessment, and Outcome Works

**Hunsley, J., & Mash, E. J. (Eds.). (2018). *A Guide to Assessments that Work* (2nd ed.).** Evidence-focused assessment across disorders and populations.

**Lambert, M. J. (2010). *Prevention of Treatment Failure*.** Outcome feedback and deterioration detection.

**Miller, W. R., & Rollnick, S. (2023). *Motivational Interviewing* (4th ed.).** Current comprehensive MI text.

**Naar, S., & Suarez, M. (2021). *Motivational Interviewing with Adolescents and Young Adults* (2nd ed.).** Developmental, family, and systems adaptations.

**Rollnick, S., Kaplan, S. G., & Rutschman, R. (2016). *Motivational Interviewing in Schools*.** Brief conversations for attendance, behaviour, learning, and health.

## Research and Teaching Works

**Ambrose, S. A., et al. (2010). *How Learning Works*.** Seven research-based principles for teaching.

**Biggs, J., & Tang, C. (2011). *Teaching for Quality Learning at University* (4th ed.).** Constructive alignment and assessment design.

**Brookfield, S. D. (2017). *Becoming a Critically Reflective Teacher* (2nd ed.).** Power, student perception, and reflective teaching.

**Hattie, J., & Timperley, H. (2007).** “The power of feedback.” *Review of Educational Research, 77*(1), 81–112. Distinguishes feedback about task, process, self-regulation, and self.

**Weisz, J. R., et al. (2017).** Youth psychotherapy evidence syntheses and meta-analyses. Important for understanding modest average effects, heterogeneity, and implementation.
