## F3. Five-Part Formulation Worksheet

**Presenting pattern:** When, where, with whom, how often, impact?

**Predisposing factors:** Development, temperament, health, learning, relationships, culture, prior adversity and coping.

**Precipitating factors:** Recent changes, losses, transitions, demands, conflicts.

**Perpetuating factors:** Avoidance, reinforcement, beliefs, arousal, family/school sequences, accommodation, insomnia, practical barriers, discrimination, service fragmentation.

**Protective factors:** Relationships, skills, interests, school/community resources, values, prior successes, practical assets.

**One-sentence collaborative formulation:** “When ______ happens, I tend to ______ because ______; this helps by ______ but keeps ______ going. I already have ______ that we can build on. First we will ______.”
