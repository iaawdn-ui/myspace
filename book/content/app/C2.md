# Appendix C2: Intervention Selection Matrices

## Matrix 3: Presenting Pattern to First Questions

| Presentation | Ask first | Possible mechanisms | Do not miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| School refusal | Which lessons, routes, people, times? What happens after staying home? | Avoidance, bullying, panic, learning gap, family accommodation, sleep, transport | Safeguarding, medical issues, SEND/access, unsafe school conditions |
| Aggression | What precedes escalation? What does it stop or obtain? | Escape, status, pain, impulsivity, trauma state, communication limits | Weapon risk, abuse, neurodevelopment, mood disorder, adult escalation |
| Self-harm | Current intent, plans, means, functions, supports | Affect regulation, shame, interpersonal crisis, trauma, depression | Medical care, coercion, online harm, access to means |
| Low mood | Sleep, activity, reward, relationships, loss, future | Withdrawal, anhedonia, grief, conflict, illness, isolation | Suicide risk, bipolar features, substances, eating, thyroid/medical review |
| Aggression at home only | What happens after school? Who sees what? | Restraint collapse, family sequence, sensory load, safety cues | Domestic abuse, carer exhaustion, sibling risk |
| Silence/non-speaking | Where, with whom, since when, what helps? | Anxiety, language, trauma state, communication difference, protest | Selective mutism assessment, hearing/language, coercion |
| Eating restriction | Medical status, history, body image, control, family meals | Anxiety, sensory issues, eating disorder, GI illness, trauma | Urgent medical risk, growth, electrolytes per medical advice |
| Substance use | Function, pattern, peers, supply, consequences | Coping, belonging, dependence, trauma, exploitation | Withdrawal, county-lines/exploitation, polysubstance risk |
| Perfectionism | What error means, checking, sleep, feedback | Shame, cognitive rigidity, family/school contingencies, OCD | Eating, self-harm, obsessive-compulsive disorder, burnout |
| Friendship crisis | Sequence, interpretations, digital context, support network | Mentalizing collapse, dispute, exclusion, identity, bullying | Image sharing, coercion, suicidal response |

## Matrix 4: Mechanism-to-Intervention Guide

| If the main mechanism is… | Consider… | Pair with… |
|---|---|---|
| Avoidance negatively reinforced | Graded exposure and response prevention | Family accommodation reduction, school plan |
| Low reward and withdrawal | Behavioural activation | Sleep, social connection, academic triage |
| Low mastery/self-efficacy | Graded tasks, coping models, feedback | Social cognitive planning |
| Skill deficit | Instruction, modelling, rehearsal, homework | Generalisation in real settings |
| Escalating family sequence | Parent coaching, communication analysis | School alignment, safety plan |
| Shame-based identity story | Narrative externalisation | Affirmative support and system change |
| Mentalizing collapse | Stop–rewind–explore, affect naming | Crisis plan and team consistency |
| High autonomic arousal | Grounding, orienting, movement, sleep | Trauma phase assessment |
| Interpersonal dispute | IPT-A communication and decision analysis | Family/school negotiation |
| Ambivalence | Motivational interviewing | Practical barrier removal |
| Grief | Grief support, IPT-A, narrative and family rituals chosen by family | School flexibility and memorial boundaries |
| Structural harm | Advocacy, consultation, safeguarding, service coordination | Individual coping only as adjunct |

## Matrix 5: Evidence and Implementation Questions

For every intervention selected, answer:

1. For which age group and problem was it tested?
2. Were young people like this one excluded?
3. What training and supervision are required?
4. How many sessions and what setting?
5. What adverse effects or burdens exist?
6. What outcome will be measured, by whom, and when?
7. What family, cultural, linguistic, disability, or digital adaptations are needed?
8. What would make us stop or refer?

A model is not implemented merely because its name appears in a plan. Fidelity, dose, sequencing, and context are part of evidence-based practice.

## Matrix 6: Session Focus Selector

| If the young person arrives… | First priority may be… |
|---|---|
| In immediate danger | Safeguarding, medical or crisis action |
| Highly aroused | Regulation, safety, reduced demand |
| Blank or dissociated | Orientation, choice, gentle pacing |
| With an urgent practical problem | Problem-solving and advocacy |
| Ready to review homework/exposure | Specific feedback and mastery attribution |
| After a family rupture | Repair, sequence analysis, safety |
| Before a known transition | Rehearsal, supports, contingency planning |
| At planned ending | Consolidation, relapse plan, transfer of supports |
