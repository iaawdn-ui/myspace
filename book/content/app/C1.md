# Appendix C: Theory Comparison Matrices

## C1. How to Use These Matrices

These matrices are revision and supervision tools, not league tables. A theory should be judged against a specific young person, problem, context, goal, evidence base, practitioner competence, and risk profile. “Best” in one row may be irrelevant or unsafe in another.

## Matrix 1: Foundational Orientation

| Theory/family | Person viewed as | Main source of difficulty | Primary change process | Counsellor role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Person-centred | Self-directing person under conditions of worth | Incongruence and denied experience | Empathy, congruence, unconditional positive regard | Facilitative witness |
| Existential | Freedom-bearing person facing finitude and choice | Avoidance of meaning, freedom, isolation, mortality | Authentic encounter and responsibility | Philosophical companion |
| Gestalt | Organism in contact with environment | Interrupted awareness and contact | Present-focused awareness and experiment | Active facilitator |
| Cognitive therapy | Information processor shaped by schemas | Maladaptive appraisal and belief | Testing and restructuring cognition and behaviour | Collaborative empiricist |
| REBT | Thinking and evaluating agent | Rigid demanding beliefs | Disputation and new philosophy | Direct teacher-coach |
| Behaviour therapy | Learner in environments | Conditioning, reinforcement, avoidance, skill deficits | New learning and contingency change | Designer and coach |
| Family systems | Person in relational systems | Interaction patterns, structure, boundaries, sequences | System reorganisation | System consultant |
| Attachment-based | Relational being shaped by care | Insecure expectations and regulation difficulties | Responsive relationships and repair | Secure-base figure/coach |
| Narrative | Author within discourses | Problem-saturated stories and power | Externalisation and re-authoring | Co-editor and witness |
| Solution-focused | Resourceful actor | Problem talk and overlooked exceptions | Exceptions, goals, small steps | Curious questioner |
| Trauma-informed | Adaptive survivor in context | Threat responses and unsafe conditions | Safety, stabilisation, paced processing | Safety partner and coordinator |
| Mentalization-based | Mind interpreting minds | Mentalizing collapse under arousal | Curiosity, affect reflection, epistemic trust | Not-knowing co-thinker |

## Matrix 2: Adolescent Suitability and Cautions

| Approach | Often useful when | Adapt for adolescents by | Use caution when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Person-centred | Shame, low agency, need for trust | Add structure, family/school context, active safeguarding | Risk requires directive coordination |
| Existential | Identity, grief, choice, meaning | Use concrete language and developmental framing | Acute crisis or severe concrete thinking dominates |
| Gestalt | Avoided emotion, contact interruption | Short experiments, choice, play/media | Flooding, dissociation, body shame |
| CBT | Anxiety, depression, panic, specific beliefs | Examples, family/school practice, shorter tasks | Literacy, neurodivergence, unsafe environments not adapted |
| REBT | Rigid self-demands and frustration intolerance | Humour carefully, collaborative language | It sounds blaming or argumentative |
| Behavioural | Observable avoidance, skills, routines | Youth-chosen reinforcers, adults change contingencies | Behaviour is communication of danger or unmet need |
| Family systems | Conflict and maintaining sequences | Protect youth voice and individual space | Abuse or coercive control makes joint work unsafe |
| Attachment | Relational insecurity and carer capacity | Work through caregivers and trusted adults | Placement instability overwhelms dyadic work |
| Narrative | Shame and imposed labels | Drawings, letters, groups, school documents | Material danger is story-treated instead of changed |
| Solution-focused | Engagement and practical goals | Concrete scales and youth language | Pain is bypassed or risk minimised |
| EMDR/trauma-focused | Discrete processed trauma memories | Preparation, caregiver, short sets, stabilisation | Ongoing threat, instability, untrained delivery |
| MBT | Self-harm, relational crises, affect instability | Family/team mentalizing and crisis plans | High arousal, unsafe family interaction, jargon |
| IPT-A | Depression linked to grief/disputes/transitions | Family and school involvement, social media context | Severe risk or non-interpersonal mechanisms dominate |
| MI | Ambivalence and mandated contact | Autonomy transparency and youth values | Used manipulatively to manufacture consent |
