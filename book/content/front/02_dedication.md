# Dedication

This book is dedicated to the young people who sit, impatient and hopeful, in the chairs that the rest of us have arranged for them; and to the teachers who insist that a theory must earn its keep in the presence of a real child.

It is also dedicated to those who are not in the room at all — the parents who are frightened, the teachers who are exhausted, the probation officers, the nurses, the siblings, and the children who have learned to be difficult because it is safer than being reachable.

A theory of counseling is, in the end, a theory of what a person is capable of becoming. That is a heavy claim to make of a set of propositions about behaviour and belief, and this book tries to hold it honestly.

To the families of our students, and to our students themselves: thank you for the patience required to read a book that asks you to think before you act, and to act before you are certain.
