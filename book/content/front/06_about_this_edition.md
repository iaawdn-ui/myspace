# About This Edition

## Edition statement

This is the first edition of *Fundamentals of Counseling Theories (CAC. 701)*, prepared for use in the Master of Arts in Children and Adolescent Counseling at Triune Biblical University Global Extension. The manuscript word count of this edition, including the appendices, the glossary, and the annotated bibliography but excluding the table of contents, the lists of figures and tables, and the index, is [[WORDCOUNT]] words.

## What is new

Because this is a first edition, the appropriate question is not what has changed but what this edition contains. It provides:

- A complete survey of the major theoretical traditions in counseling, organised so that each tradition is examined in the same sequence: origins, constructs, mechanism of change, technique, application to youth, evidence, limitations, cultural and ethical demands.
- Eighty-nine original diagrams and illustrations, each prepared for this edition, covering theoretical models, clinical decision processes, developmental sequences, and comparative matrices.
- A portfolio of twelve extended composite case studies in Appendix B, each constructed to be contested by more than one theory.
- Comparative matrices in Appendix C that allow any two theories to be set side by side across twenty dimensions.
- A glossary of more than four hundred terms in Appendix D, and a curated, pathway-organised annotated bibliography in Appendix E.
- Model documentation, session and worksheet templates, supervision instruments, and referral guidance in Appendices A, F, G, and H.
- A complete teaching apparatus in Chapters 57 and 58, including a twelve-week delivery plan and an assessment blueprint with grading rubrics.

## How to cite this work

A full citation of the first edition:

> Department of Counseling and Human Development. (2026). *Fundamentals of counseling theories (CAC. 701): A graduate textbook for the Master of Arts in Children and Adolescent Counseling* (1st ed.). Triune Biblical University Global Extension.

Individual chapters may be cited by their chapter number and title, for example: "Chapter 16, 'Cognitive Therapy: The Cognitive Model,' in *Fundamentals of Counseling Theories (CAC. 701)* (1st ed., 2026), Triune Biblical University Global Extension."

## A note on the composite cases

Every case in this book is a composite. No single real individual is depicted. Details have been combined, altered, and constructed to produce situations that are clinically instructive and pedagogically useful, while protecting the privacy of everyone whose material may have informed the writing. Where a case resembles a specific person, that resemblance is coincidental.

## A note on the absence of religious content

This book is written within the secular, empirically grounded tradition of counseling theory and practice. It does not argue for, assume, or require any religious or metaphysical position, and it does not include devotional material, scriptural exposition, or confessional content. Where the worldviews and spiritual practices of a young person or family are clinically relevant, they are treated as resources and perspectives to be respected and worked with, in line with the profession's ethical requirement of cultural responsiveness. Readers whose own commitments differ are invited to supply them in their practice; readers should understand that the book is silent on such matters, not that it has adjudicated them.
