# Contributors and Acknowledgements

## The Department of Counseling and Human Development

This edition was prepared by the teaching and editorial staff of the Department of Counseling and Human Development at Triune Biblical University Global Extension. The department's graduate sequence is organised around the assumption that a counseling practitioner must be able to do three things well: think from a defensible theoretical position, gather and interpret information with adequate rigour, and build and repair a working relationship with a person who is often less powerful, younger, or more frightened than the professional. CAC. 701 is the course in which the first of these three is taught, and the standards applied in its preparation reflect the department's commitment to the second and third.

## Roles in the preparation of this edition

**Course convenor.** The course convenor for CAC. 701 has overall responsibility for the alignment of this text with the course learning outcomes, the twelve-week delivery plan in Chapter 57, and the assessment blueprint in Chapter 58, and for ensuring that the case material is appropriate to the graduate programmes for which it is used.

**Theory section editors.** Each of the eight parts of the book was drafted by a section editor with responsibility for the literature of that part, and every draft was read by at least one editor from outside the part. The reading of cross-part material was deliberately made adversarial: the developmental and cultural lenses were applied to the clinical chapters, and the clinical chapters were reviewed for developmental and cultural adequacy, because a theory that has been reviewed only by people who share its assumptions is not properly reviewed.

**Evidence and methods reviewer.** A reviewer with responsibility for the empirical literature has checked every quantitative claim, effect size, and reference for accuracy and appropriate strength of claim, and has advised on the distinction between statistical significance and clinical importance in the treatment of the outcome research.

**Developmental and cultural consultants.** Consultants with responsibility for child and adolescent development and for culturally responsive practice reviewed the developmental adaptations and the cultural cautions in Parts I, II, VI, and VII, and contributed the comparative matrices in Appendix C.

**Case material editor.** The composite cases were written and then stress-tested in seminar before publication, and were revised in the light of what students actually found difficult, ambiguous, or wrong.

**Production and editorial staff.** Production staff prepared the diagrams, the typesetting, the index, and the apparatus, and are responsible for the consistency of the chapter architecture, the numbering of figures and tables, and the cross-referencing.

## Acknowledgements

Individual names are not listed here because the contributors to this edition are colleagues who have asked to be identified by role rather than by name, and the department has honoured that request.
