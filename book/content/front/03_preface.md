# Preface

## The book that sits at the centre of a curriculum

Every graduate programme in counseling has one course that students remember years later, usually with a mixture of affection and horror. It is the course in which they are required to learn a dozen incompatible accounts of how human beings work, and then to decide, in front of their peers, which one they intend to use. CAC. 701, *Fundamentals of Counseling Theories*, is that course in the Master of Arts in Children and Adolescent Counseling at Triune Biblical University Global Extension. This book was written to serve it.

The premise of the book is simple and, in the seminar room, unfashionable: a counselor who has not seriously studied a body of theory is not yet a professional. The strength of a theory is not that it is true — no theory in this field is simply true — but that it is coherent, that it is testable, and that it makes a claim about how change happens that can be defended, examined, and, when it fails, revised. The student who leaves CAC. 701 able to state the mechanism of change proposed by each of the major traditions, to name the assumptions on which each rests, and to say honestly which of those assumptions they are willing to suspend in the service of a particular sixteen-year-old, has been taught something of permanent professional value.

## What this book is, and what it is not

This is a graduate textbook. It assumes that the reader has completed undergraduate coursework in counseling theories, or an equivalent preparation, and that the reader is entering a professional programme with the intention of working with children, adolescents, and families. It is written for readers who will eventually be responsible for other people's children: therapists in training, school counselors, family workers, and practitioners in the expanding mental health workforce.

It is deliberately secular in its orientation. It draws its intellectual ancestry from the empirical psychology of the twentieth and twenty-first centuries, from the clinical traditions that developed within that psychology, and from the contemporary commitment to cultural responsiveness and social justice. It does not argue for the truth of any religious or metaphysical system, and it does not require the reader to hold one. Where a young person's beliefs, worldviews, or spiritual practices are relevant to their wellbeing, this book treats them as clinically significant resources and perspectives to be honoured and, where appropriate, worked with — because ignoring a young person's framework of meaning is a clinical error, and because respecting it is a matter of professional competence rather than of theological persuasion. The ethical codes that govern the profession require the counsellor to be sensitive to the client's cultural and spiritual context, and they require the counsellor to do so without importing the counsellor's own.

It is also not a self-help book, and it is not a manual of techniques. Its purpose is conceptual. It aims to make the student a careful reader of theory, an accurate describer of the differences between traditions, and a more reflective practitioner.

## The scope of the book

The book is organised in eight parts. Part I establishes the foundations: what a theory is, what it does, where counseling theories came from, how they are evaluated, and what the developmental and cultural context of youth work demands. Parts II through VII survey the major theoretical traditions in depth — humanistic and existential, cognitive, behavioural, systemic and family, emotion-focused, trauma-informed, motivational, social-cognitive, developmental, and cultural — and, for each, address the origin of the theory, its central constructs, its technique repertoire, its application to children and adolescents, the evidence for it, its limitations, and its ethical demands. Part VIII turns to the working core of professional life: integration, formulation, assessment, termination, technology, school practice, and the teaching and assessment of the course itself.

Nine appendices provide the documentary and reference apparatus a graduate student will need: model consent and assent forms, a portfolio of extended case studies for seminar use, comparative matrices of the major theories, a comprehensive glossary, an annotated bibliography, session and worksheet templates, supervision and reflection instruments, referral and documentation guidance, and a glossary of acronyms with answers to recurring questions about the course.

Every chapter follows a consistent architecture so that a reader who has learned the architecture can move quickly through unfamiliar material, and so that a seminar instructor can assign comparable tasks across the whole book. Each chapter opens with learning objectives, presents the theory in its historical and conceptual context, works through its central constructs, describes the techniques it generates, applies it to a composite case involving a child or adolescent, reviews the evidence and the criticisms, and closes with a summary, a glossary of key terms, review questions, practice activities, and a selective reading list.

## On the composite cases

The cases in this book are composites. They are constructed from the kinds of presentations that appear in ordinary practice — school refusal, panic before examinations, self-harm, family conflict, school refusal followed by disclosure, withdrawal following a bereavement, aggression in a classroom, and so on. They are written to be taught with, and to be argued over. Where a case is resolved in a way that a particular theory would not resolve, that is deliberate and is usually noted. The point of the case material is not to model a correct answer but to make the consequences of a theoretical position visible.

## The evidence

The evidence claims in this book are made in the terms of the contemporary evidence base: randomised controlled trials, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, single-case designs, outcome monitoring, and practice-based evidence. Where the evidence is weak, thin, or contested, the book says so. Where a widely used technique has weak or absent support, the book says that too. This is a deliberate choice. Students who are taught otherwise will discover the gap in their first years of practice, and they will have to manage it alone.

## A word about the depth of the coverage

No single book can do full justice to every tradition it introduces. The book therefore treats the contemporary mainstream of counseling theory comprehensively, and it handles the specialist and emerging approaches more briefly but accurately, signposting where a student should go next. The annotated bibliography in Appendix E exists for exactly that purpose, and it is organised as reading pathways rather than as a list.

## Acknowledgement of the teaching team

This edition owes its shape to the collective judgement of the teaching staff of the Department of Counseling and Human Development, who reviewed drafts, argued about what should be cut, and insisted that every claim about outcomes should be traceable to a source. The course learning outcomes in Chapter 5 and the assessment blueprint in Chapter 58 were revised more than once in response to their questions, and the book is better for it.
