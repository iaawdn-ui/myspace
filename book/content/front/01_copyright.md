## A Note on Clinical Responsibility

This book is an educational text. It does not provide medical, psychological, or legal advice, and it does not substitute for supervised clinical training, licensure, or the applicable law of the jurisdiction in which the reader practises. Diagnosis, treatment selection, and the management of risk in work with children, adolescents, and families must be carried out by appropriately credentialed professionals who hold the relevant responsibility for the care of the person before them. The composite case material in this book is fictional and is presented for instructional purposes; it should not be used as a template for the assessment or treatment of an individual client.
